Puro Invoice Support

Help and support for Puro Invoice, an iPhone app for tracking courier work, calculating charges, organizing workdays, and generating shareable PDF invoices.

About Puro Invoice

Puro Invoice is designed to help courier drivers and owner-operators record daily work information in one place. The app can track mileage, deliveries, pickups, skids, special charges, and other work details, then calculate totals and create a PDF invoice that can be shared or emailed from your iPhone.

No account or sign-in is required to use the app's main features.

Common Troubleshooting

A work day is not saving

Confirm that the required work information has been entered, then try saving the day again. If the problem continues, close Puro Invoice completely and reopen it.

A total looks incorrect

Review the mileage, delivery, pickup, skid, and additional-charge entries for that work day. Correct any values that were entered incorrectly, then save the day again.

The PDF is not appearing

Make sure there is saved invoice data available. Try generating the PDF again after reopening the app.

I cannot share or email the PDF

Confirm that your iPhone has an available sharing option, such as Mail, Messages, AirDrop, or Files. If the share sheet does not appear, close and reopen the app and try again.

The keyboard is covering part of the screen

Dismiss the keyboard after entering a value, then scroll to the section you want to use. Rotating the phone back to portrait orientation may also help.

The app is not opening correctly

Close Puro Invoice completely and reopen it. If necessary, restart your iPhone and make sure you are using the latest available version of the app.

Privacy

Puro Invoice is designed to store invoice and work-day information on the user's device for the app's core functionality. The app does not require users to create an account to access its main features.

Sharing or emailing a generated PDF is initiated by the user through the iPhone sharing interface.
