Puro Invoice Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

Overview

Puro Invoice is designed to help users record courier work information, calculate charges, organize workdays, and generate PDF invoices on an iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information You Enter

Puro Invoice may contain information you enter yourself, such as mileage, delivery and pickup details, skids, workday information, charges, and invoice data. This information is used to provide the app's core functions.

Data Storage

Puro Invoice is designed to store its workday and invoice information locally on your device for normal app use.

The app does not require you to create an account or sign in to use its main features.

Data Collection

Puro Invoice does not intentionally collect personal information from users for advertising, profiling, analytics, or sale to third parties.

The app does not operate a developer-managed user account system or server-based database for the app's main invoice and work-tracking features.

PDF Sharing

Puro Invoice can generate PDF files from information you enter. Sharing or emailing a PDF is initiated by you using the sharing options available on your iPhone.

Any information you choose to send through Mail, Messages, AirDrop, Files, or another service may then be handled according to the privacy practices of that service.

Third-Party Services

Puro Invoice may rely on standard Apple iOS features, such as the system sharing interface and file handling. Apple services and any third-party apps you choose to use are governed by their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Puro Invoice is a business and productivity tool and is not specifically directed toward children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.
